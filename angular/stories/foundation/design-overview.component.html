01Foundations

Brand foundations

Procheck is a KMS Technology sub-brand. It inherits the Sun Mark, the Poppins typeface and the blue palette, then adapts them to dense, daily-use software.

Design principles

Four ideas guide every decision — from a button radius to a whole screen.

Modern

Clean, bright and flat. Borders and rules separate surfaces; one confident blue carries action.

Professional

Precise and calm. Typography, alignment and states are consistent so you can trust what you see.

Enterprise

Built for clients, audits and long projects: clear status, traceable evidence, no surprises.

Studio

A studio, not a form. Sidebar, topbar, canvas and Assistant stay put while the work changes.

60 / 30 / 10 color rule

Neutrals carry the interface, blue carries meaning and action, accents are rare moments of delight and identity.

60%Neutral
30%Primary blue
NeutralWhite and cool gray: canvas, cards, text and borders.
Primary blueElectric Blue, Dark Blue, Sky: primary actions, links, active navigation, focus.
AccentsMagenta, Purple, Teal: phase lines, chips and charts — never large areas, never gradients.

Imagery & surfaces

Bright, minimal and optimistic — the Sun Mark stands for brightness and growth.

  • Bright by default. The light theme uses a white canvas on a cool gray app background.
  • No solid black. The dark theme uses deep navy surfaces instead of pure black, as the brand guide advises against black backgrounds.
  • Mountains & resilience. The mark symbolizes resilience; keep empty states and splash areas calm and uncluttered.
  • Bold, clear, purposeful. Copy and layout speak like an approachable partner — see Voice & microcopy.

03Logo

Logo

The Sun Mark and KMS wordmark are the only brand artwork. Use the supplied files; never redraw, recolor or distort them.

Primary, reversed and mark

Primary color on white; reversed white on brand blue; the mark alone for small spaces such as the sidebar brand and the app icon.

In-app lockup. Procheck appears as the Sun Mark in a white tile, the “Procheck” wordmark in Poppins SemiBold and “by KMS Technology” as a quiet caption.

Clear space

Keep free space equal to the height of the “S” (x) on every side. Nothing — text, edges or imagery — enters this zone.

  • x = height of the “S” in KMS. Measure from the edge of the artwork, not the file’s canvas.
  • Minimum size (UI). Full logo at least 96px wide; the mark alone at least 16px. The sidebar brand mark is 24px.
  • Backgrounds. White, light neutrals or brand blue only. Never on busy imagery or dark/black fills.

Do & don’t

Never alter proportions, colors or orientation.

Primary logo on white
Reversed logo on brand blue
Mark alone in compact spaces
Don’t stretch or squash
Don’t recolor
Don’t place on busy imagery
Don’t use on black or dark fills
Don’t rotate or add effects

10Dark theme

Dark theme

Deep navy, never black. Colors are lifted just enough to keep their meaning and contrast.

Principles

How the dark theme is derived from the same semantic tokens.

  • Navy surfaces. App #0A0E18 → sidebar #0C111C → canvas #0E1320 → card #131A2A; each layer is slightly lighter to show depth.
  • Brand blue, tuned for dark. In dark, --brand is #1F6BEA, so white text on buttons stays at AA (≥ 4.5:1). Hover lifts to #2F7DFF; brand text uses #7FB2FF and links switch to Sky #62CCFE.
  • No pure black. The sidebar, topbar and overlays are tinted navy, following the brand’s guidance against solid black backgrounds.
  • Tints, not fills. Soft backgrounds become low-opacity tints of the status color; accents are brightened for legibility.
  • Chrome follows the theme. The sidebar and topbar use --bg-sidebar and --bg-topbar, so they turn light or navy with the theme, like native chrome beside the OS title bar. No part of the chrome is fixed navy.

Checklist

Quick rules to apply to every screen.

  • Targets. Buttons are 26px (dense) to 42px; icon-only buttons need aria-label and a tooltip.

12Voice

Voice & microcopy

Procheck speaks like a bold, clear, purposeful and approachable partner — a colleague who helps you get from idea to impact.

From Idea to ImpactTừ ý tưởng đến tác động

Voice principles

Four traits from the KMS brand voice.

Bold

Lead with the answer or the next step. Confident verbs, no hedging.

Clear

Short sentences, plain words. Say what happened and what to do.

Purposeful

Every word earns its place. Explain the why only when it helps a decision.

Approachable

Warm and human, never robotic or jokey. Address the user as “you”.

Writing rules

Apply in English and Vietnamese.

  • Sentence case for buttons, titles and menus. Capitalise only proper nouns such as Procheck and Claude Code.
  • Verb first on actions: “Upload documents”, “Promote to PR”.
  • What happened + next step in errors. No codes in the headline; put them in the details.
  • Use numbers and names: “14 files”, “LP-142” — not “several files”.
  • Keep technical terms such as worktree, agent, PR and HOME in English inside Vietnamese sentences.
